Zepto My Café | Case Study Overview

Executive Summary & Business Objective

Goal: 12% → 25% Repeat Rate

Zepto Café delivers snacks and beverages rapidly, yet many users repeat the exact same search and selection process for every order—resulting in a 12% 30-day repeat order rate. "My Café" transforms Zepto Café into a habit-driven daily companion targeting a 25% 30-day repeat order rate within 6 months.

1. Research Insights & Pain Points
  • • 67% Repeat Behavior: Users frequently reorder the same snacks/beverages (e.g., Masala Chai, Punjabi Samosa, Cold Coffee) but face repeated search friction.
  • • No Routine Personalization: Ordering habits across Morning, Evening, and Night slots are unsupported.
  • • Group Order Friction: Office professionals placing group snack orders face manual coordination and payment splitting hassles.
2. Phased Solution Architecture
  • ✓ Phase 1 – Smart Reorder: One-tap reordering of frequently purchased items (e.g., "Mon–Fri 10:30 AM Masala Chai") to cut checkout time.
  • ✓ Phase 2 – Snack Routine: Time-based personalized recommendations for Morning (6 AM–12 PM), Evening, and Night without rigid subscriptions.
  • ✓ Phase 3 – Collaborative Team Orders: Create a shared cart via link/QR code where colleagues add items and settle item-wise payments individually.
